What this project provides

TikTokVideoDirector is an experimental local Windows tool maintained through the Adrian22575 project repository. It prepares narrated vertical videos and captions for the owner's review. This website provides information, not a hosted video-generation service or a guarantee of public access to the application.

Review before sharing

AI-generated scripts, claims, captions and visual choices can be inaccurate or unsuitable. Review the script and final video, ensure the intended meaning is preserved, and make any necessary corrections before sharing. Source searches and local tests do not guarantee factual accuracy or publication suitability.

Content and permissions

Use only text, footage, voices and other material that you are entitled to use. Check the applicable licenses and obtain any permissions needed for uploaded material. Do not submit confidential information that you are not authorized to share with the configured providers. Follow the rules of TikTok and each provider you use.

Optional transfer to TikTok

TikTok connection depends on the platform's approval, API availability and your account authorization. The app requests only basic account information and permission to upload drafts. It does not publish directly or schedule posts.

Each transfer requires your confirmation of the account and final video. Complete editing, add any music, paste the caption and decide whether to publish inside TikTok. A successful transfer is not a published post. Disconnecting cannot recall content already accepted by TikTok.

Costs and availability

The configured API providers may charge for generation, analysis or other operations. You are responsible for your provider accounts and their charges. App estimates can differ from provider billing and unknown charges may remain unknown. The tool's ordinary creation flow does not impose a monetary cap. No payment is collected by this informational website.

This project is under development. Features, provider access and external approvals may change or fail. Keep backups of work you need to retain. These pages do not replace the separate agreements and policies of GitHub, OpenAI, ElevenLabs, Pexels or TikTok.

Questions and changes

The current project information and change history are available in the repository. Questions can be raised through the public issue tracker; do not post secrets or private content. Material changes to these terms will be reflected in this page's update date.
